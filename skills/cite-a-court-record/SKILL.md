# Cite a court record

Use this skill when making a factual claim based on litigation materials.

1. Start with CourtListener / RECAP. Search the case number and confirm the court and full case number in the docket and PDF header. If no public copy exists, consult PACER or an official court/government copy. Do not fabricate a URL or bypass access controls.
2. Fetch and open the actual document, not just a search result. Check the ECF header, document title, filing date, and relevant page. Check later orders for corrected, stricken, sealed or superseded versions. Preserve redactions.
3. Cite **court; case number; ECF/docket number (including attachment); filing date; page; direct public URL**. Specify whether the page is the court-stamped page or printed internal page. If they differ, give both. A docket entry's filing date may differ from the date signed.
4. State what kind of record supports the claim: an allegation in a complaint, testimony, a charge in an indictment, a verdict, or a court finding. Publication and unsealing do not validate everything a record contains. Cite a judicial ruling for a claim about what a court decided.
5. Link CourtListener's document or verified RECAP PDF. When an order refers to an earlier entry, inspect that entry before describing it. If fetching fails, mark it unverified and omit dependent claims; do not guess a docket or attachment number.
6. Do not reproduce victims' or accusers' names, private addresses, passenger lists, or connect uncharged living people with wrongdoing. Retain only a case caption necessary to locate the litigation. Review a quote for identifying information before sharing it.

Citation form:

> [Court], No. [case number], ECF [entry]-[attachment if any], filed [YYYY-MM-DD], p. [court-stamped page] ([document type]), [verified public link].

Verified example: S.D.N.Y., No. 1:15-cv-07433-LAP, ECF 1319, filed 2024-01-03, p. 1 (order), [CourtListener / RECAP](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.nysd.447706/gov.uscourts.nysd.447706.1319.0.pdf). This page states that unsealed filing would begin that day with exceptions; it does not adjudicate the truth of the underlying statements.

For visual artifacts, keep these citations in the adjacent machine-readable data and list every source and supported claim in SOURCES.md. Show this disclaimer:

Being named in a document is not evidence of wrongdoing. Sources are linked for every entry.
